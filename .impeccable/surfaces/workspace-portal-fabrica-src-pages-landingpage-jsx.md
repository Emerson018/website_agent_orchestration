# Portal Aether: public lead intake page

Scope: the public page of the Portal (workspace/portal_fabrica): header, first viewport with the intake form, Como funciona, Diferenciais, Trabalhos, footer. Backend, form fields, validation and the POST to /api/leads stay as they are.

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owners of local businesses (restaurants, barbershops, clinics, cafés) anywhere in Brazil. Action: send the intake form (name, company, e-mail, WhatsApp, optional site), or talk on WhatsApp (51) 98447-0914. Proof: three real sites (20barranove, Campeiro Fogão, Marcianos) shown as screenshots, no links, no metrics. Constraints: no prices, deadlines, invented cases or urgency; plain Portuguese with "você"; persuasion only from true statements.

## Direction contract

THESIS: every point of light is a real detail of the client's business (Maps, reviews, photos, menu); the site is what connects them. Refuses the headline-over-three-cards landing.

OWN-WORLD: near-black #050507 ground; bone-white mesh ink; signal red #EF4444 only for the action and awakened points; deep violet #1B004B as depth fields behind sections. Cormorant Garamond display (pinned by the user's hero), Plus Jakarta Sans text. Hairline 1px rules, no cards, no glass; sections are open fields.

STORY: the owner sees their business as raw material made visible, believes the site is built from it, sees three real sites, sends the form or opens WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the Ecos de Luz canvas full-bleed, sculpture anchored right; left column: Aether wordmark, a three-line display headline, one line of subcopy, the working form directly below in a black slab with a hairline frame, red primary button, WhatsApp as the quiet secondary. Phone: sculpture centered behind a dark fade, headline then form.

FORM: user-pinned direction (the Ecos de Luz hero), no roll. seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: the pointer awakens the mesh; motion grammar: one slow fade-up of the first-viewport copy, nothing else animates.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
