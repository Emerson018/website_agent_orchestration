# Pricing: Implantação plus Mensalidade per Tipo de Projeto, sold alone or in a Combinação

Each Tipo de Projeto has a one-time Implantação and a Mensalidade, because the Agenda and the Dashboard keep running on paid hosting and Supabase after delivery. Buying two or more types is a Combinação at a fixed price below the sum, with the saving shown in reais. The public page shows "a partir de" prices and the operator closes the final value in the proposal. The Dashboard is sold only with the Agenda, since it reads the Agenda's numbers, and it is offered in the proposal, not on the portal form.

## Decisions

| Tipo | Implantação | Mensalidade | Prazo alone |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| Site | R$ 1.490 | R$ 59 | 7 dias úteis |
| Agenda | R$ 1.990 | R$ 89 (1 profissional), R$ 129 (2–5), R$ 179 (6+) | 10 dias úteis |
| Dashboard (only with Agenda) | R$ 990 | R$ 49 | 7 dias úteis after the Agenda |

| Combinação | Implantação | Saving | Prazo |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| Site + Agenda (badge "Recomendada") | R$ 2.990 | R$ 490 | 15 dias úteis (Site on day 6) |
| Agenda + Dashboard | R$ 2.590 | R$ 390 | 15 dias úteis (Agenda on day 10) |
| Site + Agenda + Dashboard | R$ 3.690 | R$ 780 | 20 dias úteis (Site day 6, Agenda day 15) |

- The discount applies only to the Implantação; Mensalidades add up. A type added later by an existing client gets the Combinação price.
- What each Implantação includes (stated on the public page): Site, one page with up to ~8 sections and a catalog of up to 60 items, WhatsApp button, map, hours and photos; Agenda, services and professionals registered, configurable hours, the owner panel; both with 2 rounds of adjustment. Copywriting from scratch, professional photos and items beyond the limits are quoted apart.
- Prazos count from the day the client's material arrives. A Combinação is faster than the sum because the Design do Cliente is made once.
- Implantação: Pix, 50% on approving the proposal and 50% on delivery, 5% off; or card in up to 10x sem juros at full price, received month by month (anticipation only when needed and approved).
- Mensalidade: the first month is included; billing starts 30 days after delivery, by Pix Automático or card. A notice goes out at 15 days late; at 30 days the project goes offline and the client gets its files.
- No lock-in: the domain is registered in the client's name, and on cancellation the client receives the project files (and the Supabase data export for Agenda and Dashboard).
- Payments go through Asaas: Pix in the same link, card installments for a CPF account, subscriptions, and invoices later.
- Formalization: the operator sells as a pessoa física (contract, receipt, carnê-leão) and opens an ME in the Simples Nacional after the second sale. MEI is not used: building sites and software is generally not an allowed MEI occupation. Confirm the CNAE and the Fator R with an accountant before opening.
- Persuasion uses only true statements: real Combinação savings, the Mensalidade per day, the Pix discount, "10x sem juros", the first month included and "sem fidelidade". No fake "de/por" prices, scarcity or countdowns. "Mais escolhida" replaces "Recomendada" only once it is true.

## Considered Options

- One-time price only: rejected, the operator would pay hosting forever or charge too much upfront.
- Monthly only, no setup: rejected, the operator would finance the build and lose it on an early cancellation.
- No public prices: rejected, the operator would spend WhatsApp time on leads who cannot pay.
- Lock-in contract: rejected in favor of "o site é seu", which is a true selling point.
